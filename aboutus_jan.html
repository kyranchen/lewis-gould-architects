Jan Gould

Principal · AIA, NCARB

Jan Gould enjoys being involved in all aspects of architecture from conceptual design through construction. He is presently working with net-zero projects which will produce as much energy as they use.

Among Jan's expertise are state and local planning reviews, working with multiple stakeholders and cross word puzzles.

Jan enjoys the challenge of solving several problems with a single solution. His love of travel, history and his work as a sculptor inform his design.

Jan earned his professional degree from Pratt Institute and holds an NCARB certification to practice countrywide.
