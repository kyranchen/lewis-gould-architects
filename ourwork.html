Our Work

Steel framing for Tribeca Penthouse

Our work is creative yet practical; cost effective, energy efficient and respectful of our environment.

We have always worked on a wide variety of project types, scales and budgets. Projects include new construction, renovation and restoration and adaptive re-use.

From a child's room to components for a celebration at Rockfeller Center to proto-type design for a pre-fab store that simply plugs in, we are problem solvers.

Full architectural services can be provided from site selection, through construction documents to finished interiors. We are also used as a design team for building and facility managers for planning and engineering offices.

One partner is personally involved with each project from beginning to end. Highly creative staff and sure-footed consultants form dynamic teams allowing us to handle large and complicated projects.

One of our strengths is the co-ordination of the many people, trades and agencies involved in successfully completing a project.

Most of our work over 25 years is from our broad base of repeat clients and their referrals.
