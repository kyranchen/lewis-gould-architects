Michele Lewis

Principal · AIA

Michele enjoys the range and possibilities in architecture. She is as comfortable with land planning and affordable housing as with finely detailed interiors and furniture. Her passion for color, view and light bring a painter's eye to each project. Michele's roots are in renovation and adaptive reuse. Early works in community projects eased her way into settings for green initiatives for town and schools. From urban planning to private spaces, Michele understands the unique importance of every space; she finds the heart of each project and offers her sensitive perspective to the user and her craft.
